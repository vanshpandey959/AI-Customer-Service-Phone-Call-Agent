## About Us
Chandigarh Cafe and Restaurant is a multi-cuisine family cafe and restaurant in Sector 17, Chandigarh. We opened in 2014 and have been serving fresh, quality food for over twelve years.
We serve North Indian and Punjabi food, Chinese, Continental, pizza and pasta, burgers and sandwiches, and a full cafe menu of tea, coffee, shakes and desserts.
We offer a breakfast menu, lunch, an evening cafe menu, dinner, and a Sunday brunch buffet.
We have dedicated Jain and vegan menus, and separate cooking stations for veg and non-veg food.
Our head chef is Chef Harpreet Bedi, who specialises in Punjabi and tandoori cooking.
Most guests are families, college students, office groups and tourists visiting Sector 17.
We do not serve alcohol.

## Timings

### Main Outlet — Sector 17 Hours
The main outlet is open all 7 days of the week, from 8:00 AM to 11:30 PM, Sunday to Thursday.
On Friday and Saturday the main outlet stays open until 12:00 midnight.
Kitchen last order is 30 minutes before closing time.
Breakfast is served from 8:00 AM to 11:30 AM every day.
Lunch is served from 12:00 PM to 3:30 PM. The full menu is available at lunch.
Evening cafe time is 3:30 PM to 7:00 PM. Only the cafe menu is served: starters, snacks, sandwiches, burgers, pizza, pasta, desserts and beverages. Main course dishes are not served in this window.
Dinner is served from 7:00 PM to 11:30 PM, and until 12:00 midnight on Friday and Saturday. The full menu is available at dinner.
Sunday brunch buffet runs from 11:00 AM to 3:30 PM.

### Holidays and Special Days
We are open on all public holidays with regular timings.
On Diwali day we close early, at 6:00 PM.
Any other change in timings is announced on our Instagram page and on WhatsApp.

### Home Delivery Hours
Home delivery from the main outlet runs every day from 11:00 AM to 10:30 PM.
Breakfast items are not available for home delivery.

## Reservations and Seating
Walk-ins are always welcome.
Table reservations are recommended on weekends, Friday to Sunday, and on festival days. Peak time is 8:00 PM to 10:00 PM.
Reservations can be made by calling +91-172-4567890 or on WhatsApp at +91-98765-43210, between 10:00 AM and 10:00 PM. We do not have an online table booking app.
A reserved table is held for 15 minutes after the booking time.
For groups larger than 10 people, please call at least 4 hours in advance so we can arrange seating.
Seating: indoor air-conditioned hall for 70 guests, an open-air terrace for 30 guests, and a private dining room for up to 20 guests.
The terrace depends on the weather and is not available during rain.
Private dining room: for 12 to 20 guests, with a minimum spend of Rs. 10,000 on weekdays and Rs. 14,000 on weekends and festival days. An advance of Rs. 2,000 is taken at booking and adjusted in the final bill.
Birthday and anniversary table decoration is available for Rs. 1,500, with 24 hours advance notice.
Outside cakes are allowed for birthdays on prior intimation, at no extra charge.
